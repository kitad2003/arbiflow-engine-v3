ArbiFlow Engine 4.69.0 — Incremental / Streaming Discovery

ARCHITECTURE CHANGE
- A qualified venue curve enters an in-memory edge cache as soon as that curve finishes.
- Candidate detection runs immediately from the updated cache rather than waiting for all curves.
- Candidate emission is restricted to cycles touching the changed edge.
- Emitted candidates are frozen/deep-copied and duplicate emissions are suppressed.
- The cross-venue validator accepts emitted candidates immediately while other discovery workers continue.
- Route locking from 4.65.2 is preserved.
- Conservative Base gas screen and $15 minimum-net-profit screen from 4.66.0 are preserved.
- The existing full dynamic discovery endpoints are preserved for A/B diagnostics.

NEW TELEMETRY
- curvesTotal / curvesCompleted
- quotesAttempted / quotesSucceeded / quoteFailures
- usableEdges / rejectedCurves
- candidatesEmitted / duplicateCandidatesSuppressed
- firstCandidateAt / firstCandidateLatencyMs
- validationQueueDepth
- firstValidationAt / firstValidationLatencyMs
- currentJobs / lastProgressAt / elapsedMs

ENDPOINTS
- /api/multivenue/stream/start
- /api/multivenue/stream/status
- /api/validator/crossvenue/start?limit=10
- /api/validator/crossvenue/status

SAFETY
Read-only. executionEligible=false. mainnetBroadcast=false. fundsMovedOnMainnet=false.
No approvals, signatures, swaps, or flash borrowing. Atomic executor gas estimation and
atomic simulation remain gated behind an economics survivor.
