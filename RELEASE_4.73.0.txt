ArbiFlow Engine 4.73.0 — Global Coverage Architecture Layer

Purpose
- Preserve the deployed 4.72.1 bounded Base discovery engine.
- Introduce explicit target architecture for 15 major CEX feeds, 500+ DEX/liquidity sources, and 15 Tier-1 chains.
- Separate target/registered/adapter-ready/live coverage so the API never presents roadmap counts as live market coverage.
- Add /api/global-coverage/status and /api/global-coverage/targets.
- Lock gas policy to FAIL_CLOSED for qualification when gas is unknown.

Important
- This release does NOT claim 15 CEX feeds, 500+ DEX sources, or 15 chains are live.
- Base remains the only LIVE_DISCOVERY chain in this milestone.
- Existing 4.72.1 discovery, 1inch, financing, candidate, and safety paths are preserved.
- Mainnet execution remains disabled.
